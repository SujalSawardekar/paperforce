Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Project Name
Paperforce India LLP Corporate Website

Project Overview
Paperforce India LLP requires a premium corporate website that establishes the company as a trusted notebook and paper stationery manufacturer serving international B2B markets.
The website should communicate manufacturing capability, export expertise, quality assurance, infrastructure, and long-term reliability while generating qualified enquiries from global buyers.
Unlike traditional stationery websites, the platform should emphasize industrial manufacturing, global logistics, production quality, and business partnerships.

Project Goal
Design and develop a modern corporate website that:
Positions Paperforce India as a genuine manufacturer 
Builds trust among international buyers 
Showcases manufacturing infrastructure 
Displays product portfolio professionally 
Highlights export capability 
Generates B2B enquiries 
Improves search visibility through SEO 
Creates a premium global brand presence 

Business Objectives
Increase qualified B2B leads
